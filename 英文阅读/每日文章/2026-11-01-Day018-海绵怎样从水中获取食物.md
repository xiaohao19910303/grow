## 2. 英文学习正文

### A

Sponges are animals, although they are often mistaken for plants. They come in many colors, shapes, and sizes. Most live in the ocean, but some species also live in fresh water. Like corals, adult sponges usually stay attached to a surface. However, the two groups have different body structures and feeding methods.

### B

Water is central to a sponge's life. It passes through small openings in the sponge's body and brings food and oxygen. Tiny structures inside the animal help create water currents. The sponge takes food from the passing water and releases waste. This process allows the animal to get what it needs while staying in place.

### C

Sponges also affect the water around them. As they filter water and collect bacteria, they can influence water quality on a reef. Their bodies are supported by skeletons that suit their habitats. Some live on rocky surfaces, while others live on softer sand or mud. A shared name does not mean that all sponges have the same home.

### D

Follow the pronouns as you read. In the first sentence of paragraph B, the topic is water; the next sentence begins with It. In paragraph C, them and they refer to sponges. This process points back to an action, rather than to just one object. Finding these links helps connect the sentences.

## 4. 逐段中文翻译

### A｜海绵是动物

海绵是动物，虽然它们常被误认为植物。它们的颜色、形状和大小多种多样。多数生活在海洋中，但也有一些种类生活在淡水中。像珊瑚一样，成年海绵通常附着在一个表面上。不过，这两类动物有不同的身体结构和取食方式。

they 指 sponges；the two groups 指海绵和珊瑚。most 不等于 all；adult 限定成体，避免把固定生活概括到整个生命周期。

### B｜水流送来食物

水对海绵的生活至关重要。水穿过海绵身体上的小孔，带来食物和氧气。动物体内的微小结构帮助形成水流。海绵从流经的水中获取食物，并排出废物。这一过程让动物在停留原地的同时获得所需。

首个 It 指 water；句末 what it needs 中的 it 指 the animal。This process 概括前文水流经身体、获取食物和氧气并排出废物的过程。

### C｜不同的生活环境

海绵也会影响周围的水。当它们过滤水并收集细菌时，可能影响礁区的水质。适应其栖息环境的骨骼支撑着它们的身体。有些生活在岩石表面，另一些生活在更软的沙地或泥地上。有共同的名称，并不意味着所有海绵都有相同的家园。

them、they、their 都指海绵；others＝other sponges。softer 对比前面的岩石表面，不是说所有海绵身体都更软。

### D｜跟着代词读

阅读时追踪代词。B 段第一句的话题是水，下一句以 It 开头。C 段中，them 和 they 指海绵。This process 回指一个过程，而不只是某个物体。找出这些联系，有助于把句子连接起来理解。

本段及 C 段末句为自编阅读提示与归纳。

## 6. 语法与句子拆解

### 重点一：are mistaken for——谁被误认成谁

> Sponges are animals, although they are often mistaken for plants.

- 主句：Sponges＋are＋animals。
- although 从句：they（海绵）＋are mistaken for（被误认为）＋plants；often 表频率。
- 译法：海绵是动物，虽然常被误认为植物。
- 易错点：mistaken 是 mistake 的过去分词；不要译成“植物被误认为海绵”，也不要漏掉 are。
- 迁移：The sound is mistaken for thunder. 这个声音被误认为雷声。

### 重点二：are supported by——动作的方向

> Their bodies are supported by skeletons that suit their habitats.

- 主语 Their bodies；被动谓语 are supported；by skeletons 表示支撑者。
- that suit their habitats 修饰 skeletons：适合其栖息环境的骨骼。本课先找出主干，不必背从句术语。
- 译法：适应栖息环境的骨骼支撑着它们的身体。
- 易错点：不能反过来说“身体支撑骨骼”；复数 bodies 搭配 are。
- 迁移：The roof is supported by wooden posts. 屋顶由木柱支撑。

### 复习三：代词需要结合语境

> Water is central to a sponge's life. It passes through small openings in the sponge's body and brings food and oxygen.

- 第一处主语 Water；第二句 It 回指 water，谓语 passes 与 brings 共用主语。
- 对照：Sponges also affect the water around them. them 指 sponges；不能机械地只找最近的名词 water。
- 易错点：同一个 it 在不同句子中可以指不同对象；this process 可以概括前面的多项动作。
- 迁移：The children opened the box. It was heavy. They carried it together. 孩子们打开箱子。箱子很重。他们一起搬它。
